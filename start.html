The Core Edge

Six core circuits. Stronger on defense, harder to knock off the ball.

When to do these

Off-season: 2 or 3 times a week. In season: 1 or 2. Add one to a strength or balance workout, do it on an off day before mobility or stretching, or after a running day.

Never right before a practice or a game.

Play or List?

Every workout has both. Play walks you through one exercise at a time and runs the plank timer and the rest timer for you. List shows the whole workout on one page. Switch any time.
